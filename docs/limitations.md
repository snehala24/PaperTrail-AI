# Known Limitations & Boundary Conditions

## 1. Document Ingestion Limitations
- **Scanned / Image-Only PDFs**: PyMuPDF extracts programmatic text layers. PDFs consisting entirely of scanned bitmap images without embedded OCR text will generate warnings in `Document.extraction_warnings`. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is intentionally deferred past the MVP.
- **Complex Multi-Column Math / Inline Equations**: LaTeX mathematical symbols in two-column IEEE/ACM layouts may occasionally be extracted in non-linear reading order if bounding box heuristics fail.

## 2. Chunking Heuristics
- **Section Detection**: Section headers are detected via regex heuristics (e.g. `1. Introduction`, `Abstract`, `Methodology`). Papers with idiosyncratic non-standard heading styles may have chunks grouped under generic headings.
- **Cross-Page Table Spans**: Large tables spanning multiple consecutive pages are currently partitioned per-page rather than merged into a single atomic table chunk.

This partition comprises 18 single-paper questions (Q-011–Q-028; 20 gold entries) and 2 cross-paper questions (Q-029 and Q-030; 4 gold entries, `GOLD-031`–`GOLD-034`).
  - **Cross-Paper Question Category (Q-029 and Q-030; 4 gold entries)**: Comparative and apparent-conflict queries authored by annotator snehala24 with `status: "human_draft"`. Gold evidence spans (`GOLD-031`–`GOLD-034`, also `status: "human_draft"`) ground both questions across LEMS (`doc_c67573ef50cc`) and GFWSVD (`doc_7fdb1200e823`). - **Single-Paper vs. Multi-Paper Evaluation Scope**: The automated batch retrieval benchmark (`backend/app/eval/run_retrieval.py`) is grounded exclusively in single-paper queries from the 34-page LEMS arXiv paper (`doc_c67573ef50cc`). - **Sample Size ($n=23$ Evaluated) & Confidence Intervals**:
  - Across the 30 total questions, the retrieval evaluation runner (`backend/app/eval/run_retrieval.py`) evaluates **$n=23$ answerable queries** (15 human-draft + 8 AI-draft) and reserves **5 unanswerable queries** (3 human-draft + 2 AI-draft). The 2 cross-paper queries (Q-029, Q-030) are excluded from batch runner runs.
  - This $n=23$ retrieval evaluation supersedes the initial 10-query smoke test ($n=8$ answerable evaluated, 2 unanswerable reserved; Recall@5 95% CI: [0.625, 1.0]).
  - While sample size has expanded from the initial smoke test, the evaluated population ($n=23$) remains below the standard threshold for formal statistical significance ($n < 30$).
  - As reflected in the combined retrieval evaluation artifact (`eval/runs/run_20261027_143158.json`), the 95% bootstrap confidence interval for Recall@5 under hybrid reranking is **[0.8696, 1.0]**, which serves as a directional baseline rather than a definitive statistical claim.

## 5. Day 4 Cross-Paper Boundary Conditions
- **Asymmetric Cross-Paper Term Overlap**: When querying across multiple papers where Paper A cites or reviews Paper B (e.g. LEMS reviewing GFWSVD), Paper A naturally has higher lexical and semantic term overlap for cross-paper comparative queries. Balanced candidate allocation and multi-document retrieval pooling are applied to prevent evidence starvation from the earlier paper.
- **Source-Aware Grounding vs. Secondary Description**: Claims regarding a specific paper's method should ideally be grounded directly in that paper's own primary chunks rather than in a competing paper's commentary. When primary-source evidence is absent from the cited pool, the system qualifies the response to clarify that claims rely on secondary descriptions rather than independent primary-source evidence.
- **Cross-Paper Comparison Derivation & Unresolved Relations**: When comparing claims across multiple documents, semantic consensus (`supports`), divergence (`apparent_conflict`), or direct contradiction (`contradicts`) must never be inferred solely from distinct document IDs. When the model or evidence does not establish a definitive relationship between verified multi-document claims, the relationship remains unresolved rather than defaulting to agreement or conflict.

## 7. Remaining Limitations & Future Work
- **Automated Multi-Paper Batch Runner Integration**: Queries Q-029 and Q-030 remain excluded from automated batch runs (`run_retrieval.py` and `run_conflicts.py`) due to legacy hardcoded single-paper filtering conditions. Updating the batch evaluation runners to support multi-document queries is deferred to future work.
- **Annotation Verification Graduation**: Benchmark questions and gold evidence spans remain drafts (`status: "ai_draft"` and `status: "human_draft"`) and have not yet undergone expert human verification to graduate to `status: "human_verified"` (the status string `pending_second_paper` does not exist in the dataset).
- **Upstream Gemini Free-Tier Rate Limits**: The Gemini Free Tier enforces strict quotas (20 requests/day, 5 requests/minute for Gemini 3.6 Flash). Under quota exhaustion (HTTP 429), PaperTrail AI's zero-tolerance verification policy fails closed safely by abstaining rather than emitting unverified claims. Production deployments require standard paid quotas or local fallback NLI models.
- **Corpus Scaling and Ingestion Modalities**: Cross-paper balancing and minimal claim formulation have been verified on the combined 2-paper corpus (53 total pages, 209 chunks across GFWSVD and LEMS). Performance on corpora with $>2$ papers, very large multi-document context pools, or noisy OCR scanned documents remains unproven and out of scope for Day 4.
